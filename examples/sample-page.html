Here's what most people don't realize

The bitterness and the stomach burn don't come from the coffee bean. They come from heat. Hot water pulls out the bean's sharpest acids and harshest compounds in about four minutes.

Think of it like searing versus slow-cooking. Searing is fast and loud. Slow-cooking pulls out the deep flavor and leaves the harsh edges behind.

Most store-bought "cold brew" cuts corners: a few hours of steeping, then a heat-concentrated syrup. You get the label without the method.
